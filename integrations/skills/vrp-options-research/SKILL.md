Read vrp://methodology/greeks. Discover arguments with dashboard_catalog. Query greeks_summary, greeks_signals, vrp_snapshot and regime_predict as needed.
Call-positive/put-negative exposures are model conventions, not observed dealer books. ETF gamma sign cannot be inherited by its constituents. Preserve expiry, timestamp, stale flags and provider limitations. A Greek exposure signal is not a calibrated probability of profitable trading.
State a prior market hypothesis, test it against exposures and volatility evidence, and describe the updated hypothesis with uncertainty. Distinguish option volume from verified aggressor-side flow. Do not infer bid/ask execution volume from candles.
